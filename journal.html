Cohort 01 · 13–23 July 2026

The journal

Eleven days from Bressanone to the bottom of Alpe d'Huez. Four on foot through the Dolomites, then the long road west. Every day, as it happened.

339kmTotal distance
+11,960mTotal ascent
11 daysOn the move

Day by day

Eleven days, one line

Prologue
Monday 13 July

Bressanone

Bikes stashed in Bolzano, packs weighed, an early night in Brixen. Nothing had started yet.
The Run · Stage 1
Tuesday 14 July

Plose → Rifugio Puez

A gondola does the first 1,000 metres. Everything after that is earned.
The Run · Stage 2
Wednesday 15 July

Puez → Castiglioni Marmolada

The longest climbing day of the run, and the one with cables on it.
The Run · Stage 3
Thursday 16 July

Castiglioni → Passo Valles

The biggest distance of the four, and the only cash machine on the route.
The Run · Stage 4
Friday 17 July

Passo Valles → San Martino

The shortest stage, the highest point, and a gondola down to the finish.
The Ride · Stage 1
Saturday 18 – Sunday 19 July

Bolzano → Stelvio → Bormio

Forty-eight hairpins to 2,758m, on legs that had just run for four days.
The Ride · Stage 3
Monday 20 – Tuesday 21 July

Bormio → Gavia → Lake Como

Narrower, quieter and meaner than the Stelvio. Then a lake, and a day off.
The Ride · Stage 4
Wednesday 22 July

Como → Oulx → Briançon

A travel day with one alpine col bolted onto the end of it.
The Ride · Stage 5
Thursday 23 July

Briançon → Alpe d'Huez

Over the Lautaret, down to Bourg d'Oisans, and stop at the bottom.
Epilogue
Thursday 23 July

Earn the view

Eleven days, two disciplines, one line drawn across the Alps.
